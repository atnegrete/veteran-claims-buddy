Account deletion

Draft. Scaffold only. Counsel must review this before publication.

Effective date: To be set at launch.

You can delete your Veteran Claims Buddy account in the app. Before you confirm, the app explains that deletion removes your case data, uploaded files, generated documents, and symptom history after a waiting period.

The 30 days

Deletion is scheduled for 30 days after you confirm. During that time you can sign in only to cancel the deletion and restore the account. Normal claim work does not continue while deletion is pending.

If you do not cancel, we then delete the Firebase account, the claim file, uploads, and generated documents. Billing and fulfilled Print & Mail records may be kept for accounting, without the medical file contents.

Print & Mail already paid

A Print & Mail order that was already submitted to the provider still prints and mails. That fee stays non-refundable.

Premium

Deleting the account does not by itself cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel Premium in the store or on the web billing page so it does not renew.
