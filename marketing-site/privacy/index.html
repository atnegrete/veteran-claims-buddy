Privacy Policy

Draft. This is a scaffold for Veteran Claims Buddy LLC. It is not a final privacy policy. A lawyer must review it before it is published.

Effective date: To be set at launch.

Veteran Claims Buddy LLC ("we") provides Veteran Claims Buddy, an independent guide for organizing VA disability-claim information. We are not part of, affiliated with, or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Contact: support@veteranclaimsbuddy.com

A street address is not published yet. We will provide one to Apple or Google if a store review requires it.

Who this is for

The service is offered to people 18 and older in the United States. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email support@veteranclaimsbuddy.com and we will delete it.

Information you give us

  • Account email and password.
  • Phone number, used only to send sign-in security codes. We do not send marketing text messages.
  • Profile, service history, conditions, claim information, and other answers you enter.
  • Files you upload, such as medical records and decision letters.
  • Symptom and health entries, if you use Premium.
  • Support messages you send us.

Do not type a Social Security number, full bank account number, password, or sign-in code into Vet Buddy chat. If a form needs a Social Security number or VA file number, that step is separate, and the value is stored encrypted for form generation.

Information collected automatically

  • Purchase records for a Digital Claim Package, Premium, and Print & Mail.
  • Crash reports and limited product analytics, such as whether a screen was opened or a purchase step was viewed.
  • Analytics and crash reports must not include condition names, diagnosis text, document text, claim outcomes, Social Security numbers, VA file numbers, or chat contents.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, keep the sign-in secure, store the claim file you build, prepare forms, show next steps, process payments, print and mail a package you order, answer support requests, and understand which parts of the product are used.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who else receives it

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud host the account, database, files, and security texts.
  • Google Cloud AI (Gemini on Vertex) processes documents and assistant requests you submit, inside that cloud project. Do not assume a public model is trained on your file. The contract with Google controls that, and this draft does not restate it.
  • Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat process digital purchases.
  • Stripe processes Print & Mail payments.
  • A print-and-mail provider receives the package and the mailing address only for an order you pay for. The provider is named in the order before you pay.
  • We share information if the law requires it, or to protect the service and its users.

We do not send your package to the Department of Veterans Affairs. You submit it yourself.

How long we keep it

We keep account and claim information while your account is open. If you delete the account, there is a 30-day period when you can cancel the deletion. After that, we delete the claim file, uploads, generated documents, and symptom history. We may keep billing and fulfilled-order records needed for accounting, without the medical file contents.

Your choices

You can delete the account in the app. You cancel Premium through Apple, Google, or the web subscription page where you subscribed. You can email support@veteranclaimsbuddy.com with privacy requests.

Security

Sign-in requires your email, password, and a text-message code. No method of storage is perfect. This policy does not promise that unauthorized access cannot happen.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app or by email.
