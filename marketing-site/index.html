Independent claim workspace

Organize the claim. See the next step.

Veteran Claims Buddy is an AI-powered guided workspace that helps Veterans understand disability claims, organize evidence, prepare VA forms, and know what to do next.

Veteran Claims Buddy is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Get started How it works

01

One step at a time

The claim opens on the current step, then the next. You are not handed every form at once.

02

You confirm the record

Upload a PDF, JPEG, or PNG. Each statement stays unreviewed until you confirm, correct, or set it aside.

03

You submit it yourself
