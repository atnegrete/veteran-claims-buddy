Pricing

The workspace

Organizing the claim, questions, documents, exams, and the rating calculator stay available without a purchase. Buying is not open in the app yet.

Digital Claim Package

A one-time purchase unlocks digital package generation for one Case. The business target price is $250. The store may show the closest allowed price, such as $249.99.

That purchase stays with that Case. A different Case needs its own purchase, or an active Premium subscription.

Premium

Premium is $30 a month. While it is active, it includes digital package generation for eligible Cases, symptom tracking, and monthly symptom summaries.

When Premium ends, generation stops on Cases that were never purchased. Files already generated stay downloadable. New symptom entries stop until Premium is active again.

Print and Mail

Print and Mail is optional and separate. You see one amount before you pay. That amount includes the provider's quote, a $9.95 service fee, and card processing. Those parts are not listed as separate charges. The charge is non-refundable once paid. It does not file the claim.

We do not promise a rating, an approval, or any other VA outcome. Refunds for the Case unlock and Premium follow the store where you paid.
