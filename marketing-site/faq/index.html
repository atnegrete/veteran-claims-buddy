FAQ

What Veteran Claims Buddy does, and what it does not file.

Do you file the claim with the VA?

No. You submit it on VA.gov or by mail.

Can a spouse or survivor use this?

No. The account and the forms are for the Veteran whose disability claim this is. Someone filing for a Veteran is not supported.

Are you part of the VA?

Veteran Claims Buddy is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

What does the $250 purchase cover?

A full year of Premium for one active Case. The store may show $249.99. It does not include Print and Mail.

What does Premium cover?

While Premium is active ($29.99 a month or $234.99 a year), it covers package generation for every Case, symptom tracking, and monthly symptom summaries.

Is the calculator an official VA decision?

No. It is an estimate from the published rate table. It does not apply the bilateral factor.
