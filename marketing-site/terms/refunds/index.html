Refunds and cancellation

Draft. Scaffold only. Counsel must review this before publication. Store rules override any sentence here that conflicts with Apple, Google, or Stripe.

Effective date: To be set at launch.

Digital Claim Package and Premium

Refunds for the $250 Case unlock and for Premium follow the Apple, Google Play, or Stripe process for the place you paid. We do not promise a refund because of a VA decision, a rating, or a delay.

Canceling Premium stops the next renewal. It does not erase Cases, generated files, or symptom history. The current paid period continues until it ends, as the store reports it.

Print & Mail

Print & Mail is non-refundable once paid. The charge is separate from the Digital Claim Package and from Premium. See the Print & Mail terms.

How to ask

Email support@veteranclaimsbuddy.com. If you paid Apple or Google, you may also have to request the refund in that store.
