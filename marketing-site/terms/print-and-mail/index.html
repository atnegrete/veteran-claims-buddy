Print & Mail terms

Draft. Scaffold only. Counsel must review this before publication.

Effective date: To be set at launch.

Print & Mail is optional. It is not included in the Digital Claim Package or in Premium. It is a separate Stripe payment on iPhone, Android, and the web.

We send your finalized package to a print-and-mail provider. The provider prints it in black and white, on one side of each page, and mails it to the United States address you confirm. You still review, sign where required, and submit the package to VA. Buying Print & Mail does not file the claim.

Price

You see one Print & Mail amount before you pay. That amount includes the provider's live quote for printing and postage, a $9.95 service fee, and card processing. Those parts are not listed as separate charges at checkout. The amount changes with page count and mail service. We do not promise a fixed shipping price.

Refunds

The Print & Mail charge is non-refundable once paid.

Your address

You enter the mailing address for that order. We do not keep a profile address as the default destination. You must confirm the address before payment.

Privacy

The provider receives the package file and the mailing address for that order. The provider is identified before you pay. See the privacy policy.
