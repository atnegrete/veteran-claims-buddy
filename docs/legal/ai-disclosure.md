# AI and data use

**Draft.** Scaffold only. Counsel must review this before publication.

**Effective date:** To be set at launch.

Veteran Claims Buddy uses automated tools, including Google Cloud AI (Gemini on Vertex), to:

- read documents you upload and suggest structured facts;
- answer questions in Vet Buddy;
- draft text, such as a lay statement or a letter for your doctor to review;
- summarize symptom entries you confirmed.

You decide what becomes part of your Case. The assistant does not submit claims, set deadlines, or decide that a fact is true. Suggested facts stay suggestions until you confirm them.

Check VA and medical information before you act. Do not type a Social Security number, full bank account number, password, or sign-in code into chat.

A doctor draft is a draft for a clinician. It is not a medical opinion from Veteran Claims Buddy, and it is not a nexus opinion until a qualified clinician signs it.

Document text and questions you submit may be processed by Google Cloud inside our cloud project. This page does not claim that Google trains public models on that data. The Google agreement controls that processing.

Product analytics do not include chat text, document text, or condition names. See the privacy policy.
